Aagahi
Lifestyle-based future health risk awareness

What could your habits today mean for your health years from now?

Answer a short set of questions about your daily life, and see how your risk of common lifestyle-related conditions compares to average — now, and as you get older.

This is a screening and awareness tool, not a medical diagnosis. It estimates patterns from population health data — always speak to a doctor for a real clinical assessment.

Your risk trajectory

If your current habits stay exactly the same as you age.

This trajectory compares your current habits to people with similar lifestyles observed at different ages in the dataset. It assumes your habits don't change — which means the reverse is also true: changing your habits now can change this path.

Aagahi is a prototype awareness tool built for demonstration purposes. Predictions are based on general population data and general clinical guidance, not on data specific to any single country or community.
